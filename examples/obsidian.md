# Obsidian demo

## Callouts

> [!note]
> No title: the name of the type is the title.

> [!abstract] Chapter summary
> A custom title, with **bold** text.

> [!todo]
> Something to do.

> [!success]
> It worked.

> [!question]- Folded by default
> The answer shows on a click.

> [!failure]+ Open, can be folded
> A failure.

> [!danger]
> Danger.

> [!bug]
> A bug.

> [!example]
> An example.

> [!quote]
> A quotation.
